What We Do

Our Dyeing & Finishing Services

End-to-end textile processing solutions engineered for export-quality results.

Stenters

High-capacity stenter machines for heat setting, width fixation, drying, and finishing of all fabric types. Ensures dimensional stability for export-grade cloth.

  • Width setting & fixation
  • Heat setting for synthetics
  • Drying & chemical padding

Pressurized Jiggers

High-temperature pressurized jigger machines for dyeing polyester and synthetic blends. Achieves deep, uniform shade penetration with superior fastness.

  • Polyester & synthetic dyeing
  • High-temp pressure processing
  • Consistent shade matching

Normal Jiggers

Versatile open-width jigger machines for reactive and vat dyeing of cellulosic fabrics. Ideal for small and medium batch dyeing with precise colour control.

  • Cotton & cellulosic fabrics
  • Reactive & vat dyes
  • Batch dyeing flexibility

Sanforizing

Mechanical pre-shrinking process that guarantees dimensional stability. Essential for export fabrics to meet international shrinkage standards.

  • Residual shrinkage control
  • Soft hand-feel finishing
  • International standard compliance

Washing

Industrial continuous washing ranges for thorough scouring, washing-off after dyeing, and removal of unfixed dyes and chemicals for clean, bright results.

  • Scouring & desizing
  • Post-dyeing wash-off
  • Soaping & neutralizing

Continuous Bleaching

Open-width continuous bleaching range for preparing grey fabric to bright, clean white. Optimized for cotton and cotton-blend fabrics.

  • High whiteness index
  • Cotton & poly-cotton blends
  • Consistent quality across lots

Rotary Printing

Multi-colour rotary screen printing for vibrant, detailed designs on fabric. Suitable for high-volume production with fast repeat accuracy.

  • Up to 12-colour printing
  • Reactive & pigment inks
  • High-speed production runs

Singeing

Gas singeing machines to remove protruding fibres and surface fuzz from fabric, resulting in a clean, smooth surface ideal for printing and finishing.

  • Fabric surface smoothing
  • Improved print definition
  • Double-side singeing

Mercerizing

Caustic soda treatment for cotton fabrics that enhances lustre, tensile strength, and dye absorption for richer, more vibrant results.

  • Increased fabric lustre
  • Improved dye uptake
  • Enhanced tensile strength

Continuous Dyeing

High-speed continuous pad-dry-cure dyeing ranges for large-volume production. Delivers uniform shade, excellent fastness, and efficiency for export orders.

  • Pad-dry-cure process
  • Large-volume production
  • Excellent wash fastness

Our Process

From Grey Fabric to Finished Roll

A seamless processing flow under one roof — reducing lead times for exporters.

01

Singeing & Desizing

Surface fuzz removal and starch removal from grey fabric.

02

Continuous Bleaching

Whitening and preparing fabric for dyeing or printing.

03

Mercerizing

Caustic soda treatment to increase lustre, strength, and dye uptake of cotton fabric.

04

Dyeing / Printing

Jigger, continuous dyeing or rotary printing as required.

05

Washing & Soaping

Thorough wash-off for clean, bright, residue-free fabric.

06

Stenter Finishing

Width setting, drying, and application of finishing chemicals.

07

Sanforizing

Final pre-shrinking for dimensional stability before dispatch.
