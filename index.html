About Us

Dyeing & finishing since 1995

Shamim Textile Mills PVT LTD was established in 1995 at 4km, Manga-Raiwind Road. For three decades we have operated as a dyeing and finishing textile unit — converting greige woven fabric into coloured, processed, finished cloth for garment factories, home textile makers, exporters and fabric traders.

Our work covers washing and pretreatment, fabric dyeing, pigment and rotary printing, mechanical finishing, shrinkage control, and final fabric preparation — cotton, PC (poly-cotton) and woven fabrics under one roof.

Our Services

Dyeing & finishing services

Specialised services that colour, wash, finish and prepare woven fabric for garment, home textile and trading customers.

Fabric Dyeing

Piece dyeing of woven fabric after weaving — cotton, PC and blends coloured to customer shade requirements.

  • Piece dyeing (woven fabric)
  • Batch dyeing on jiggers
  • Continuous dyeing for volume
  • Reactive dyeing for cotton
  • Disperse dyeing for PC / polyester
  • Vat dyeing for deep, durable shades

Washing & Pretreatment

Preparation of greige fabric so it is clean, absorbent and ready for dyeing or printing.

  • Singeing & desizing
  • Scouring & bleaching
  • Fabric washing & soaping
  • Neutralization
  • Optical brightening (as required)

Printing

Print designs on woven fabric grounds for apparel and home textile applications.

  • Pigment printing
  • Rotary screen printing
  • Multi-colour production runs

Fabric Finishing

Mechanical and chemical finishing that sets width, hand-feel and appearance of the cloth.

  • Stentering (width & heat setting)
  • Dry cans / cylinder drying
  • Softening & hand-feel finishes
  • Heat setting for synthetics / PC

Shrinkage Control

Dimensional stability so finished fabric meets buyer shrinkage standards before garment making.

  • Sanforizing
  • Residual shrinkage control
  • Improved fabric stability

Quality & Dispatch Support

Supporting steps that help customers approve shade and receive fabric ready for the next stage.

  • Shade approval & sampling
  • Fabric inspection
  • Batch preparation
  • Rolling & packaging
  • Customized processing as required
